# E & A Cosmetics — Part 3 Website

E & A Cosmetics (Edith & Abby) was established in 2022.

## Website pages
- `index.html` — Home
- `about.html` — About Us
- `products.html` — Dynamic product collection with search, category filter and sorting
- `gallery.html` — Product gallery with click-to-enlarge lightbox
- `enquiry.html` — Product/service enquiry form
- `contact.html` — General contact form and Google Maps location

## Part 3 functionality
- Responsive mobile navigation.
- JavaScript-generated product cards, live search, category filtering and sorting.
- Gallery lightbox (close using ×, outside click or Escape).
- Client-side form validation and accessible error feedback.
- Forms prepare pre-filled emails using the visitor’s email application.
- Footer year updates automatically.

## Form submission note
This is a static website. Forms validate in the browser and open a pre-filled email to `edithandabby@gmail.com`. The visitor must press **Send** in their email application. Direct website submission without opening an email app requires a form service or server endpoint.

## SEO
Page-specific titles and descriptions, semantic headings, descriptive image alt text, internal links, local business structured data, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, responsive layouts and lazy-loaded images.
